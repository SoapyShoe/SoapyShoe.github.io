DOS PUEBLOS ENGINEERING ACADEMY · 2021–2022

Anti-gravity cone

Taking a physics exhibit from CAD toward a fabricated prototype.

I worked on a six-person team to build an anti-gravity cone exhibit from an existing CAD design. We fabricated a base structure using 80/20 aluminum extrusion, brackets, and routed wooden components.

My main contribution was the ramp assembly. I worked from CAD through an acrylic prototype, laser-cut the ramp profiles, and drilled and threaded the mounting holes. Cracking acrylic and assembly mistakes meant revising the process and working more carefully through each step.

I also helped cut and check the aluminum extrusion and prepare brackets and drawings. We presented the unfinished prototype at the DPEA engineering showcase in May 2022. The project taught me how much careful fabrication and assembly matter when turning a CAD model into a physical exhibit.
