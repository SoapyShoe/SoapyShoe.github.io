02

TOYON RESEARCH CORPORATION · 2025 — 2026

A robotic end-effector
for motor manufacturing

An automated motor assembly project, developed through months of independent research and iteration.

CADMotor controlRaspberry PiFabrication

During my internship at Toyon Research Corporation, I created a fully automated end-effector for a motor-manufacturing robotic arm. I worked through the research, design, fabrication, integration, and testing needed to turn the initial concept into a working system.

I used CAD to develop the mechanical assembly and integrated motors, drivers, sensors, and control hardware. The design went through repeated rounds of 3D printing and machining until the system operated reliably.

What I took from the build +

Months of independent research, design, iteration, and testing taught me a myriad of skills in CAD, Raspberry Pi programming, motor control, 3D printing, and machining. I learned to understand how the mechanical, electrical, and software pieces interact, and to debug the full system until it works.

03

UCLA · ELEGANT MIND CLUB

A custom drone
with two eyes

Building the hardware for a drone project exploring stereo vision, object recognition, and a virtual reality interface.

SolidWorksRaspberry PiPythonComputer vision

In the Elegant Mind Club at UCLA, I worked on a custom quadcopter with two cameras. The project explored capturing two views of motion and using object recognition and optical flow to investigate depth.

I designed the chassis components in SolidWorks, using 3D-printed supports and a laser-cut acrylic platform. The supports went through several iterations to account for acceleration, deceleration, and the possibility of a sudden drop.

Hardware and software +

The hardware combined RS2205 motors, Arducam cameras, a Raspberry Pi, a LiPo battery, and a Pixhawk flight controller. The software work explored ArduPilot Mission Planner, camera feeds to a VR headset through Unity and C#, and YOLOv5 for object recognition. Testing also involved a Tello drone.
