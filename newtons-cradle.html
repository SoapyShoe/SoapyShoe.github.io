DOS PUEBLOS ENGINEERING ACADEMY · 2021–2022

Newton’s cradle

Redesigning the alignment and drive mechanisms of an interactive physics exhibit.

I worked with Kira and Trisha to improve an existing, motorized Newton’s cradle for the Dos Pueblos Engineering Academy. The exhibit was assembled, but its lead screws moved slowly, parts flexed, and the suspended balls needed better alignment.

We designed and built a jig that fit the exhibit’s slot to align the balls and make future adjustments repeatable. We also replaced the lead screws to reduce friction and improve motion, and worked on the supporting plates, spacers, and motor brackets.

My work included CAD, machining and threading spacers, modifying clearance holes, and assembling the mechanism. We tested the mechanical improvements at the engineering showcase; the control code still needed further work.
