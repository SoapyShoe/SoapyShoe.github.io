TOYON RESEARCH CORPORATION · 2025 — 2026

Automating motor magnet insertion

An automated motor assembly project, developed through months of independent research and iteration.

CADMotor controlRaspberry PiFabrication

During my internship at Toyon Research Corporation, I created a fully automated end-effector for a motor-manufacturing robotic arm. I worked through the research, design, fabrication, integration, and testing needed to turn the initial concept into a working system.

I used CAD to develop the mechanical assembly and integrated motors, drivers, sensors, and control hardware. The design went through repeated rounds of 3D printing and machining until the system operated reliably.

What I took from the build +

Months of independent research, design, iteration, and testing taught me a myriad of skills in CAD, Raspberry Pi programming, motor control, 3D printing, and machining. I learned to understand how the mechanical, electrical, and software pieces interact, and to debug the full system until it works.
